Questions

Before you open an account.

Is PocketPal free to use? +

Yes. Creating an account, tracking expenses, scanning receipts, and getting your monthly report all cost nothing.

How does receipt scanning actually work? +

Upload a photo and PocketPal sends it to a receipt-reading service that extracts the merchant, total, and date. You'll always see a preview to confirm or correct before anything is saved.

Can I sign in with Google? +

Yes, or register with a username and password. Either way, your password is hashed and never stored in plain text.

What happens to a recurring bill if I miss a few months? +

PocketPal catches up automatically the next time it checks in, logging each missed occurrence in order so your history stays accurate.

Can I get my data out if I leave? +

Anytime. Export your full history or a filtered range as a CSV or PDF directly from your dashboard.
